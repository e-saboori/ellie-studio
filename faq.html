Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about working together and getting your website online.

Do you only design websites, or do you also build them?

I design and build the full website, including layout, responsive development, deployment, and launch support.

Can you help me connect my domain?

Yes. I can help connect your domain to your website and make sure it works properly.

Do you work with businesses outside Toronto?

Yes. I work with small businesses in Toronto and across Canada.

What kind of websites do you build?

I build simple custom static websites for small businesses, service providers, studios, consultants, and local brands.

What if I don’t know exactly what I need yet?

That’s okay. You can send a rough idea, and I’ll help you figure out the best next step.

How long does a website take?

A simple website usually takes about 2–4 weeks once I have your content, photos, and feedback. Timing can vary depending on the number of pages and how quickly revisions are approved.

What do I need to send you?

Send any text, photos, logo files, business details, contact information, and a few websites you like. If everything is not ready yet, send what you have and I’ll help you organize the rest.

Do I need to have a domain already?

No. If you already own a domain, I can connect it. If you do not have one yet, I can explain how to purchase one and help connect it when the website is ready.

Do you offer monthly maintenance?

Monthly maintenance is not included. These websites are built to be simple and low-maintenance, and future updates or small changes can be quoted separately when needed.

Is SEO included?

Basic SEO setup can include clear page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, clean page organization, and descriptive image alt text. Advanced SEO campaigns, ongoing keyword work, link building, and guaranteed rankings are not included.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. I can review an existing small business website and rebuild it with a clearer structure, updated design, and better mobile experience when the project fits a simple static website.

Can you add booking links or Instagram links?

Yes. I can add links to booking platforms, Instagram, social profiles, maps, email addresses, and other useful tools your business already uses.
